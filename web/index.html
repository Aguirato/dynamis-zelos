FROM PROTOTYPE TO SIGNAL

Connect your hardware.

Start with one sensor. Validate each step before expanding.

01

Choose the reference build

MyoWare 2.0 Muscle Sensor + MyoWare 2.0 Wireless Shield (ESP32). The RAW signal routes to GPIO36 through the shield.

Hardware, wiring & bill of materials ↗
02

Flash the BLE firmware

Remove the wireless shield from the muscle sensor before USB programming or charging. Follow the pinned Arduino build instructions.

Firmware & upload instructions ↗
03

Connect over Bluetooth

Use Chrome or Edge on a supported desktop with Bluetooth enabled, on localhost or HTTPS. Click Connect sensor and choose ZELOS. Repeat for each additional device.

Safari and Firefox are not supported for live Bluetooth. Demo mode needs no device.

04

Check, reference, record

Bench-test first. For body-connected acquisition use the documented battery-only assembly, with USB disconnected. Verify stream health, label the channel, then capture a stable 3-second reference.

Body-contact precautions ↗
The custom nRF52840 enclosure shown in the concept is a future engineering path. This release does not include a manufactured PCB, enclosure CAD, clinical validation, or a verified eight-sensor radio system.
